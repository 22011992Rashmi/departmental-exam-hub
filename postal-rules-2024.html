Post Office Rules, 2024

The Department of Posts publishes the Post Office Rules, 2024 in its official Acts and Policies repository. The official document states that the rules came into force on 16 December 2024.

Preparation

Learn definitions, conditions, procedures and exceptions from the official text. Example: Q—When did the Post Office Rules, 2024 come into force? Answer—16 December 2024.

Accuracy note: Old study material can become outdated. Verify the latest official Department of Posts source before relying on a rule, syllabus or exam pattern.
