POST OFFICE RULES, 2024

Post Office Rules, 2024

Overview

The Post Office Rules, 2024 are subordinate rules made under the Post Office Act, 2023. They form part of the legal framework governing postal administration under the new Act.

Exam caution: Rules and regulations can be amended. Use the latest official text when checking a rule number, limit, fee or procedure.

Core areas to study

  • Definitions and interpretation.
  • Postal articles, transmission and delivery.
  • Postage and postal charges.
  • Registration, accountable items and value-added services.
  • International postal matters and restrictions.
  • Complaints, compensation, liability and administrative provisions.
  • Operational duties, records and compliance.

How to prepare

For every rule make a four-column revision sheet: Rule number → subject → key requirement → exception/condition. Pay special attention to “shall”, “may”, provisos and exceptions.

Rules vs Regulations

The Post Office Rules, 2024 and Post Office Regulations, 2024 are related but distinct instruments under the Post Office Act, 2023. Study their provisions in their own context.

Revision checklist

  • Learn the relationship with the Post Office Act, 2023.
  • Mark frequently used definitions.
  • Record exceptions separately.
  • Maintain an amendment log.
  • Practise topic-wise MCQs after each study session.
