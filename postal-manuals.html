Postal Manuals

How to study Postal Manuals

India Post publishes multiple Postal Manual volumes and Post Office Guides. Use the syllabus to identify the relevant volume/topic.

High-yield areas

  • Mail office procedures
  • Delivery and transmission
  • Accounts and cash handling
  • Branch Post Office work
  • International mail
  • Records and returns

Revision method

For every manual topic prepare: purpose, responsible official, record/document, sequence, exception and reporting requirement.
