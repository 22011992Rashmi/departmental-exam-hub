POSTAL OPERATIONS

Postal Operations

Study map

Cover booking, acceptance, classification, sorting, transmission, receipt, delivery, returns, accountable items, complaints and records. Learn the workflow from acceptance to final delivery.

Booking

Focus on acceptance conditions, addressing, postage/charges, service selection, receipts and restrictions.

Transmission and sorting

Study segregation, sorting, dispatch, receipt and correct routing. Distinguish transmission-stage work from delivery-stage work.

Delivery

Revise identification of the addressee or authorized person, delivery evidence and treatment of undelivered articles.
