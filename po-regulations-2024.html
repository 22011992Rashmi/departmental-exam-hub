PO REGULATIONS, 2024

PO Regulations, 2024

Overview

The Post Office Regulations, 2024 provide detailed regulatory provisions under the Post Office Act, 2023. The principal regulations were notified on 16 December 2024 and came into force on the same date.

Currentness note: The regulations have subsequently been amended. A 2026 official notification records a Third Amendment effective 1 April 2026. Always verify the latest text before relying on a numerical limit or specific clause.

Key study framework

  • Preliminary provisions and definitions.
  • Postage, stamps and postal articles.
  • Domestic mail and accountable items.
  • Delivery, receipt and authorized persons.
  • International post and customs-related provisions.
  • Complaints, compensation and liability.
  • Schedules and prescribed particulars.

Important definition: accountable item

The official definition connects an accountable item with a booking receipt for track-and-trace and evidence of delivery through the signature of the addressee, payee or authorized person, for the categories specified in the regulation.

Operational reading method

For every regulation identify who acts, what is done, when, what record/evidence is required and what exception applies.

Amendment discipline

Keep an amendment table with notification date, provision affected, change and effective date. This is especially important for rates and limits.
