Inspector Posts LDCE – Complete Preparation Guide

A practical, topic-wise roadmap for candidates preparing for the Limited Departmental Competitive Examination for Inspector Posts.

Source status: Based on the Department of Posts revision dated 22 August 2025 and corrigendum dated 24 September 2025. Always verify the latest official notification before an examination.

1. Latest examination pattern

The revised pattern provides for three papers. Paper I carries 250 marks, Paper II carries 50 marks and Paper III carries 300 marks.

Important: The Department of Posts states that the examination is without aid of books.

2. Qualifying marks and applicability

The current pattern specifies a minimum of 40% in each paper with an overall average of 45% for General Category candidates. For SC/ST/PwBD candidates, it specifies 33% in each paper with an overall average of 38%.

It also states that circulars, orders and guidelines issued up to 31 December of the year preceding the examination year are part of the syllabus, subject to the exceptions stated in the official document.

3. Paper I syllabus

Paper I is a 250-mark objective paper covering postal law, savings, insurance, manuals, operations, technology, employee rules and departmental instructions.

Acts

  • Post Office Act, 2023
  • Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873
  • Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and relevant AML/CFT amendments
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019
  • Information Technology Act, 2000

Rules & Savings Schemes

  • Post Office Rules, 2024
  • Post Office Regulations, 2024
  • Government Savings Promotion Rules, 2018
  • POSA, RD, TD and Monthly Income Account schemes
  • Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme, NSC and Kisan Vikas Patra
  • PPF and Sukanya Samriddhi Account schemes

Postal insurance & manuals

  • Postal Life Insurance Scheme, 2011 and amendments
  • Book of BO Rules
  • Specified Postal Manual volumes
  • Specified mail operations and money remittance material
  • Post Office Guide and domestic/foreign post guidelines

Operations, savings & technology

  • Jansuraksha schemes
  • Mail and Parcel Network Optimization guidance
  • Centralized delivery and Dak Ghar Niryat Kendra guidance
  • POSB manuals and subsequent SB Orders
  • Department of Posts Annual Reports and Book of Information
  • APT Knowledge (IT 2.0), CBS, PLI-CIS and India Post Payments Bank

Service & conduct rules

  • Preservation and disposal of postal records
  • CCS (Conduct) Rules, 1964
  • CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965
  • CCS (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965
  • GDS (Conduct & Engagement) Rules, 2020 and relevant instructions

4. Paper II – Noting, Drafting & Charge Sheet

Paper II is entirely written and is therefore different from the objective papers.

Noting – 15 marks

Practise concise official notes: facts, applicable rule/provision, analysis and a clear proposal or conclusion.

Drafting – 15 marks

Practise formal departmental communication with precise, factual and appropriately structured language.

Major Penalty Charge Sheet – 20 marks

Learn the structure and drafting principles from the applicable service rules and departmental instructions. Do not rely on an outdated template.

Practice method: Write Paper II answers under the one-hour limit. Check structure, rule references, clarity, chronology and whether the answer addresses the case.

5. Paper III syllabus

Paper III is a 300-mark objective paper with 150 questions. It includes constitutional law, criminal procedure, administrative and financial matters, pension/NPS, workplace-related law, English, general knowledge, current affairs, reasoning and aptitude.

Constitution & law

  • Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties
  • Specified constitutional Articles listed in the official syllabus
  • Specified provisions of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
  • Central Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985
  • Revenue Recovery Act, 1890
  • Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 as amended
  • RTI Act, 2005 and RTI Rules, 2012

Procurement & financial rules

  • Manual on Procurement of Goods
  • Manual on Procurement of Works
  • Manual on Procurement of Consultancy & Other Services
  • CCS (GPF) Rules, 1961
  • CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021 and amendments
  • CCS (Commutation of Pension) Rules, 1981
  • Fundamental Rules and Supplementary Rules
  • Specified General Financial Rules and financial powers

Service matters

  • POSH Act, 2013 and related instructions
  • CCS implementation of NPS rules and gratuity under NPS rules
  • Casual labourer instructions
  • APAR-related instructions
  • Service Discharge Benefit Scheme, 2010
  • Employee and GDS welfare measures
  • Specified P&T/Postal Financial Handbook material

General section

  • English language
  • General knowledge and current affairs
  • Indian economy, polity, sports, culture and science
  • Reasoning and intelligence
  • Interpersonal skills and mental aptitude
  • Quantitative aptitude
  • Ethics

6. Preparation strategy

  1. Start with the official syllabus. Make a checklist of every topic.
  2. Build a rule notebook. Record definitions, conditions, exceptions, authorities and important amendments.
  3. Separate Paper I and Paper III. Their subject mix is different.
  4. Practise Paper II early. Do not leave writing practice for the final week.
  5. Use current official instructions. The revised pattern makes specified preceding-year circulars/orders/guidelines relevant.
  6. Practise timed MCQs. Speed matters in both objective papers.

8. MCQ strategy

MCQs on this website should be original, syllabus-linked and explained. Each question should show the correct answer and a short explanation or source reference.

Sample question

Q. Under the revised Inspector Posts LDCE pattern, how many MCQ-type questions are specified for Paper I?

A. 100   B. 125   C. 150   D. 200

Answer: B — 125. Paper I is specified as 125 MCQ-type questions carrying 2 marks each.

9. Frequently asked questions

Is this the official India Post website?

No. Departmental Exam Hub is an independent educational resource. Candidates should rely on the latest official Department of Posts notification and instructions.

Is the 2025 revised pattern applicable to future examinations?

The Department of Posts stated that the revised pattern applies to examinations held after its issue, with the September 2025 corrigendum modifying specified portions. Check the latest notice for the particular examination.

Are old notes enough?

Not necessarily. The official syllabus brings specified preceding-year circulars, orders and guidelines into scope, so older notes should be cross-checked against current official material.
