General Postal Knowledge — 50 MCQs

50 questions with answers and brief explanations.

Q1. The Department of Posts functions under the:

  1. Ministry of Communications
  2. Ministry of Railways
  3. Ministry of Agriculture
  4. Ministry of Tourism

Answer: A

Explanation: The Department of Posts is under the Ministry of Communications.

Q2. The Post Office Act, 2023 provides:

  1. the parent statutory framework for the postal regime
  2. a recruitment notice
  3. a rate chart only
  4. an attendance register

Answer: A

Explanation: It is the parent legislation.

Q3. India Post provides:

  1. mail, parcel and other postal services
  2. only railway tickets
  3. only passports
  4. only private courier services

Answer: A

Explanation: India Post provides a range of postal services.

Q4. A postal article should be handled according to:

  1. applicable service conditions and rules
  2. personal preference
  3. customer guesswork
  4. unrelated procedures

Answer: A

Explanation: Postal handling is governed by applicable provisions.

Q5. Departmental exam preparation should use:

  1. current syllabus and applicable official material
  2. only old papers
  3. only social media
  4. only memory

Answer: A

Explanation: Current official material is the safest basis.

Q6. The Post Office Act, 2023 is different from: (Practice 6)

  1. a Postal Manual volume
  2. the parent statute
  3. an Act of Parliament
  4. the legal framework

Answer: A

Explanation: A Postal Manual is a manual; the Act is legislation.
