# Departmental Exam Hub v2

Clean standalone website for Postal Departmental Exam preparation.

## Includes
- Dropdown navigation for Exams, Study Material and MCQs.
- Official Sources menu removed.
- Detailed notes for Post Office Rules, 2024 and PO Regulations, 2024.
- Additional study pages for Postal Manuals, Postal Operations, Office Procedure and Financial Rules.
- 50 MCQs each for six topics.

## Important
The Post Office Rules, 2024 and PO Regulations, 2024 have amendment history. Verify current provisions, rates, limits and syllabus against the latest official Department of Posts material before publishing or relying on a specific legal/financial detail.
