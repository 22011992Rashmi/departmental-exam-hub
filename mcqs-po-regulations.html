PO Regulations, 2024 — 50 MCQs

50 questions with answers and brief explanations.

Q1. The Post Office Regulations, 2024 were notified on:

  1. 16 December 2024
  2. 1 January 2020
  3. 18 June 2023
  4. 15 August 2025

Answer: A

Explanation: The principal regulations were notified on 16 December 2024.

Q2. The regulations were made under which section of the Post Office Act, 2023?

  1. Section 13
  2. Section 1
  3. Section 50
  4. Section 100

Answer: A

Explanation: The principal notification invokes section 13.

Q3. The principal Post Office Regulations, 2024 came into force on:

  1. 16 December 2024
  2. 1 April 2025
  3. 1 January 2026
  4. 18 June 2024

Answer: A

Explanation: The principal notification specifies 16 December 2024.

Q4. An accountable item is associated with:

  1. booking receipt, tracking and delivery evidence
  2. only ordinary untracked posting
  3. only office files
  4. only cash receipts

Answer: A

Explanation: The definition links accountability with booking, tracking and delivery evidence.

Q5. Why should rates and limits be checked against amendments?

  1. They can be amended
  2. They never change
  3. Amendments are irrelevant
  4. Only manuals can change

Answer: A

Explanation: The regulations have an amendment history.

Q6. A regulation should be studied with attention to: (Practice 6)

  1. operative requirement and exception
  2. only its heading
  3. only publication date
  4. only a coaching summary

Answer: A

Explanation: The operative requirement and exceptions are essential.

Q7. For an accountable item, delivery evidence helps establish: (Practice 7)

Q8. An administrative instruction should be used: (Practice 8)
